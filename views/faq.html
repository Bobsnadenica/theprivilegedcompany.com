Frequently asked questions.

Answers about our services, approach, pricing, and how we work together.

01

What is 1+AI?

1+AI combines a lead architect with AI-assisted development tools. The architect is responsible for technical decisions and delivery; AI supports research, implementation, and iteration.

02

Do you build custom mobile apps?

Yes. We build high-performance, native-feel web and mobile applications optimized for speed, scale, and security. We specialize in bespoke solutions for cases where off-the-shelf frameworks fall short.

03

How does SEO engineering work?

We work on site structure, loading speed, metadata, and structured data to help search engines discover and understand your content. Rankings also depend on content, competition, and other factors.

04

Can you scale a legacy system?

Yes. We review the existing architecture, use telemetry and testing to identify bottlenecks, and plan targeted improvements or migrations based on your requirements.

05

How do you handle my information?

This site uses no tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Contact briefs are stored in a private Amazon S3 inbox for us to review. If delivery fails, the form opens your email client as a fallback. We use your details to respond and scope the work, never sell them, and you can ask us to delete them. See our Privacy page for details.

06

What does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so we don't quote blind. Share what you are trying to build or fix and we come back with a clear plan and price before any work starts. One-to-one tech training is fixed at 100 euro per session.

07

Do you work under NDA?

Yes. Client work is treated as confidential, and we do not publish client names or project details in marketing without written consent.
