Privacy.

Discretion is central to how we work. Here's what we do — and don't do — with your information.

01

How We Handle Your Data

The website sets no tracking or advertising cookies and loads no third-party analytics. Our contact form stores the details you provide, including your name, email, optional phone number, and project notes, in a private Amazon S3 inbox in Ireland (eu-west-1). Access is restricted to the configured administrator. If delivery fails, the form opens your email client as a fallback. We use your details only to respond and scope the work, do not sell them, and you can request deletion at contactus@theprivilegedcompany.com.

02

Discreet Operations

For our high-net-worth and executive clients, we operate under strict non-disclosure protocols. Your involvement with ThePrivilegedCompany is a private matter. We do not use client names in marketing materials without explicit, written consent.

03

AI Integrity

Our AI-driven engineering processes are executed in isolated environments. We utilize private models to ensure that your proprietary code and data are never leaked into public training sets. Your intellectual property remains yours, exclusively.
