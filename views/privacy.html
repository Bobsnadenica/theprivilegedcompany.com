Privacy.

Discretion is central to how we work. Here's what we do — and don't do — with your information.

01

How We Handle Your Data

The website sets no tracking or advertising cookies and loads no third-party analytics. The contact form prepares an email in your own email app; you review it and press Send. The website does not upload or store your form details. When we receive your email, we use your details only to respond and scope the work, do not sell them, and you can request deletion at contactus@theprivilegedcompany.com.

Supported Facebook links can add approved source, medium, campaign, and content labels to your email draft. These labels stay in memory during this visit; after a reload, only labels in the current link are available. We do not collect advertising click IDs or include the full query string. Opening a draft does not tell us whether you sent an email.

02

Discreet Operations

For our high-net-worth and executive clients, we operate under strict non-disclosure protocols. Your involvement with ThePrivilegedCompany is a private matter. We do not use client names in marketing materials without explicit, written consent.

03

AI Integrity

Our AI-driven engineering processes are executed in isolated environments. We utilize private models to ensure that your proprietary code and data are never leaked into public training sets. Your intellectual property remains yours, exclusively.
