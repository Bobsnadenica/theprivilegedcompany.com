Data deletion.

Last updated: 27 September 2026

You do not need an account to visit this website. If you authorized the Soroya publishing application or believe the project holds personal information about you, use the steps below.

1. Disconnect Facebook access

In Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations. Find Soraia (the legacy application name) or Soroya, and remove its access. Menu names can vary; search settings for Business integrations if needed. If the connection appears under Apps and websites instead, remove it there.

This prevents further access through that authorization. It does not by itself delete existing Page posts or the project's local records.

2. Request deletion of project-held information

Email contactus@theprivilegedcompany.com with the subject Soroya data deletion request.

  • Describe the information or connection you want removed.
  • Include the relevant Page or post link, or another non-secret identifier that helps us locate the record.
  • Use an email address where we can reply. Never send a password, access token or app secret.

We will review the request, seek proportionate verification when needed, and reply with the outcome or any reason information must be retained. Requests are handled by the operator; this page does not trigger an automatic deletion job or guarantee a fixed completion time.

3. Published Facebook content

If you want a specific public post removed, identify it in your request. Deleting local records or disconnecting an integration does not automatically remove Facebook content, copies held by Meta, or copies shared by other people. You can also use Facebook's own controls to manage your comments, messages and other activity.

For details about what the application stores and why, read the Soroya privacy policy.
