Soroya · project and publishing application

Privacy policy.

Effective and last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy covers the Soroya project pages at theprivilegedcompany.com/dev/soroya/ and the application used to manage Soroya's Facebook Page. The project is operated by The Privileged Company. The Meta authorization interface may still show the application's original name, Soraia.

For privacy questions or requests, email contactus@theprivilegedcompany.com and mention Soroya.

What this website collects

These project pages are static. They have no login, contact form, analytics, advertising scripts or embedded Facebook widgets, and do not set their own cookies or browser storage. Following links to Facebook or other parts of our website opens services with their own privacy practices.

The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary technical request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement. These pages do not give us a visitor-tracking dashboard.

Information used by the publishing application

Only authorized administrators connect the Page through Meta's authorization process. The application uses the permissions pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_posts to identify the selected Page, read its recent posts and publish authorized content.

  • Page identifiers, name and connection information.
  • A Page access token and application credentials, kept in restricted local storage.
  • Recent Page post identifiers, captions, publication times and links, for editorial context, duplicate prevention and verification.
  • Generated media, prompts, captions, publication records and recovery records.

An administrator's authorization token is used during connection to obtain the Page token and is not intentionally retained afterward. The current application does not import private messages, maintain a follower-profile database, or automatically reply to comments or messages.

Why information is used and who receives it

We use this information to operate the authorized Page, publish and verify posts, prevent duplicates, recover failed operations and respond to requests. Public images and captions are sent to Meta for publication. Image-generation providers process the reference image and prompts used to create the fictional character's content; website visitors' browsing information is not included in those prompts.

Facebook interactions, including comments, likes and messages you choose to send through Facebook, are processed by Meta under Meta's privacy policy. If you contact us by email, our email service processes that correspondence. We do not sell personal information or use this application to build advertising profiles.

Storage and retention

Connection credentials and publishing records are stored in a separate local project with restricted access. Public artwork and this website's source files may also be stored in the website's public repository. Credentials and private publishing records are not included in that repository.

We retain operational records while they are needed to run and troubleshoot the project, prevent duplicate publication, or handle requests. We do not promise a fixed automatic deletion period. The stored Page token is replaced when the connection is renewed. Revoking access in Meta does not automatically erase the local credential file; its removal can be requested using the deletion instructions. Necessary records may be retained for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

Your choices and data deletion

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by this project. Available rights depend on your circumstances and applicable law. We may need proportionate information to verify that a request relates to you; do not send passwords or access tokens.

Administrators can revoke the integration through their Facebook account settings. Revoking access does not automatically erase local publication records or delete already published Facebook posts. See the step-by-step data-deletion instructions for requesting removal of project-held information or specific published content.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the project's processing changes and revise the date above. Questions can be sent to contactus@theprivilegedcompany.com.
