Meet Soroya.

A fictional adult model, created with AI. A little elegance, a little mischief, and an eye for everyday details.

Soroya explores fashion and lifestyle through generated photographs and short stories. The character and scenes are fictional; the images do not document a real person's daily life.

A consistent character.
Room to experiment.

The project combines reference-guided image generation, editorial review and Facebook Page publishing. Styling, expressions and everyday scenes change while Soroya's defining features stay consistent.

It is a creative experiment in visual storytelling and publishing automation, with human direction. It does not represent a real model's bookings, endorsements or attendance at events. Available platform labels and image provenance are preserved.

Simple and transparent.

This site explains the project. It does not ask visitors to create an account or connect their Facebook profile. The publishing application is used by authorized Page administrators.
