Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This page describes the public Mist of Atlas website and its illustrative map demo. The mobile application is in development; its data practices must be confirmed before any public release.

1. Optional location

The demo starts at a sample city center. Only selecting Use My Location requests a current position from your browser. It is used in page memory to center the maps; this website does not record a location history, upload coordinates to a company server, create an account, or synchronize a personal atlas. Reloading starts the sample demo again.

2. Map and website providers

Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap. Tile requests disclose the viewed map area and ordinary request information, including your IP address, to that provider. The page also requests fonts from Google Fonts and the Leaflet library from unpkg. These providers and our hosting service receive technical information needed to deliver those resources.

3. Local offline cache

A service worker caches this project's public page files in your browser. It does not cache third-party map tiles or store the location obtained by the demo. Clear site data in your browser to remove the local cache. Location access can be revoked in your browser's site permissions.

4. Contact

Email links open your own email app. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the message. Our team receives the information you include and uses it to respond to your enquiry.

For privacy questions or requests, contact contactus@theprivilegedcompany.com.
